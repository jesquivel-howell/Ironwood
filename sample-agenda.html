A day at Ironwood —

The Ironwood
Signature Retreat

A snapshot of how a typical day unfolds at our Finger Lakes barn — from continental breakfast to a finished welded sculpture in hand.

9:00 AMArrival & mingling

Welcome & Continental Breakfast

Kick off the day with fresh fruit, yogurt, granola, pastries, coffee, tea, juice, and water while your team settles in and gets ready for an unforgettable experience.

9:30 AMApprox. 2.5 hours

Hands-On Welding Experience

No experience necessary. Our guided welding workshop is designed for beginners and focuses on creativity, collaboration, and fun.

Your experience includes
  • Professional welding instruction
  • A custom metal art project for each participant
  • Take-home finished sculpture
  • Custom event swag — t-shirt, bandana, and sticker
  • Photos and videos of the experience shared with your team
  • A supportive, engaging environment that encourages teamwork and creativity
12:00 PM1 hour

Lunch & Team Reflection

Enjoy a catered lunch and take time to connect, reflect, and celebrate your team's accomplishments.

Sample lunch menu
  • Choice of wraps
  • Garden salad with house vinaigrette
  • Chips and cookies
  • Several alternative menu options available
1:00 PMUp to 3 hours

Team Meeting Session

Transition from the shop to focused team time in our private event space.

Meeting space amenities
  • Private use of the retreat space
  • Projector and presentation screen
  • Comfortable seating and gathering areas
  • Quiet countryside setting ideal for planning, collaboration, and team building
4:00 PMWrap up

Retreat Concludes

Head home with a one-of-a-kind sculpture, great memories, and a stronger connection to your team.

Every retreat can be customized to fit your group's goals, schedule, and budget.
